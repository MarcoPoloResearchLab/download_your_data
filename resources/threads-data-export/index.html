How to Export Your Threads Information

A Threads data export currently begins in the Instagram mobile app's Accounts Center. You select the profile whose Threads information you want, then choose the included data and device-export settings.

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

Threads does not start this workflow from a separate download page in the current guide. The route passes through the Instagram app and Accounts Center, so choosing the correct connected profile is the key step.

Current workflow

How to complete the current provider export

  1. 1

    Open the Instagram app, tap your profile picture, then open the menu in the top right.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  2. 2

    Tap Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  3. 3

    Tap Export your information → Create export.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  4. 4

    Select the profile whose Threads information you want, then tap Next.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  5. 5

    Select Export to device.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  6. 6

    Choose the specific information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  7. 7

    Tap Start export.

    Open the first-party step ↗

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Why does the Threads export start in Instagram?

The current first-party workflow uses the Instagram app's Accounts Center to reach Your information and permissions for the connected profile.

Can I choose which Threads information to export?

Yes. The flow lets you choose the specific information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

How long can a Threads data export take?

Meta may take up to 30 days to email the download link.
