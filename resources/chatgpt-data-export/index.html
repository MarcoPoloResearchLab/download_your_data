OpenAI (ChatGPT) resource

How to Export Your ChatGPT Data

A ChatGPT data export is requested from the signed-in product's Data controls. OpenAI prepares a ZIP containing conversation history and other account data, then sends a time-limited download link.

Open the visual ChatGPT export guide See the workflow

Quick verdict

Best for
Saving a copy of ChatGPT history and account data
Required input
An eligible signed-in ChatGPT account
Product fit
The public guide is available now; browser ZIP import is not yet available

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

The export is asynchronous and the final link is short-lived. Requesting the archive is only the first half of the workflow: you must return while signed in to the same account and save the ZIP before the link expires.

Current workflow

How to complete the current provider export

  1. 1

    Sign in to ChatGPT.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  2. 2

    Open your profile menu, then select Settings.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  3. 3

    Select Data controls.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  4. 4

    Under Export data, select Export.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  5. 5

    On the confirmation screen, select Confirm export.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  6. 6

    Wait for OpenAI’s email or SMS; the export can take up to 7 days.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  7. 7

    Open Download data export while signed in to the same account. The link expires after 24 hours, so save the ZIP promptly.

    Open the first-party step ↗

Objections and limits

What this resource does not promise

  • OpenAI says archive preparation can take up to seven days.
  • The download link expires after 24 hours.
  • Download Your Data does not yet accept the OpenAI ZIP through the browser workspace.

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Where is Export data in ChatGPT?

Open the profile menu, choose Settings, select Data controls, and use Export under Export data.

How long can a ChatGPT data export take?

The current first-party flow says preparation can take up to seven days.

Can I import the ChatGPT ZIP into Download Your Data?

Not through the browser yet. The current public resource covers requesting and saving the export without presenting browser import as available.
