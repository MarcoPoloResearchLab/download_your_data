How to Export YouTube Data with Google Takeout

A YouTube data export uses Google Takeout with YouTube and YouTube Music selected. The focused workflow avoids starting with an account-wide archive and still lets you refine the YouTube data included.

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

General Google Takeout starts with many products selected. The focused YouTube route narrows the first decision, then lets you review YouTube-specific options before choosing delivery and archive settings.

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Is a YouTube data export the same as a full Google Takeout?

It uses Google Takeout, but the focused route starts with YouTube and YouTube Music selected instead of requiring an account-wide export.

Can I choose which YouTube data to include?

Yes. Use the YouTube and YouTube Music data-options control before moving to the delivery step.

Where does Google send the YouTube archive?

You choose the delivery method. The guide highlights email delivery because it avoids connecting an additional external destination.
