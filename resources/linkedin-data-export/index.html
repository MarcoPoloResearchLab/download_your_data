LinkedIn resource

How to Download Your LinkedIn Account Data

A LinkedIn data export can contain selected categories such as connections or messages, or the larger account archive. Both options start under Data Privacy and Get a copy of your data.

Open the visual LinkedIn export guide See the workflow

Quick verdict

Best for
Choosing between a quick category export and the larger LinkedIn archive
Required input
A signed-in LinkedIn account on the web
Product fit
Download Your Data provides the public request guide but no LinkedIn importer

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

LinkedIn's delivery time depends on the scope you select. Specific categories can arrive quickly, while the larger archive takes longer, so choose only the data needed for the task.

Current workflow

How to complete the current provider export

  1. 1

    On LinkedIn on the web, select Me → Settings & Privacy.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  2. 2

    Open Data Privacy in the left rail.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  3. 3

    Under How LinkedIn uses your data, open Get a copy of your data.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  4. 4

    On Download my data, choose specific categories or the larger data archive.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  5. 5

    Click Request archive.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  6. 6

    LinkedIn emails specific categories within minutes and the larger archive within 24 hours.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  7. 7

    Use the email link to download the archive within 72 hours.

    Open the first-party step ↗

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Can I download only LinkedIn connections or messages?

Yes. The current Download my data screen lets you choose specific categories instead of requesting the larger archive.

How long does a LinkedIn data export take?

LinkedIn says specific categories can arrive within minutes and the larger archive within 24 hours.

How long does the LinkedIn download link work?

The current guide says to use the emailed archive link within 72 hours.
