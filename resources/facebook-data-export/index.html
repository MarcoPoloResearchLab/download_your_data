Facebook resource

How to Export Your Facebook Information

A Facebook data export begins in Accounts Center under Your information and permissions. You choose the Facebook profile, included information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

Open the visual Facebook export guide See the workflow

Quick verdict

Best for
Creating a scoped copy of Facebook posts, photos, messages, and profile data
Required input
A signed-in Facebook profile with access to Accounts Center
Product fit
Download Your Data provides the public export guide but no Facebook importer

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

Accounts Center supports multiple profiles and many export options. Confirm the Facebook profile and scope before starting so the completed archive contains the intended information without unnecessary data.

Current workflow

How to complete the current provider export

  1. 1

    Log in on the web. Profile menu → Settings & privacy → Settings.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  2. 2

    Open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  3. 3

    Click Create export, then select the Facebook profile to export.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  4. 4

    Select Export to device.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  5. 5

    Choose the information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  6. 6

    Click Start export.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  7. 7

    When Facebook notifies you, use Available downloads within 4 days to retrieve the protected export.

    Open the first-party step ↗

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Where is Facebook Export your information?

Open Accounts Center, choose Your information and permissions, then select Export your information.

Can I choose what goes into the Facebook export?

Yes. The current flow lets you choose the profile, information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

How long is a Facebook export available to download?

Facebook places the completed export under Available downloads for four days.
