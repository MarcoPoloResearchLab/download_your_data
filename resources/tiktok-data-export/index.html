How to Request and Download Your TikTok Data

A TikTok data export starts in the mobile app under Settings and privacy, Account, and Download your data. You choose the included information and format before submitting the request.

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

TikTok separates requesting data from downloading the prepared file. The archive may take several days, and the completed file remains available only for a limited window.

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Can I request TikTok data from the web?

This current guide follows TikTok's mobile app path through Profile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Account, and Download your data.

How long does a TikTok data export take?

TikTok says preparation may take a few days and sends an in-app notification when the file is ready.

How long is the TikTok download available?

The prepared file remains available for up to four days, so download it promptly after the notification.
