Instagram resource

How to Export Your Instagram Information

An Instagram data export starts from the web product's Settings and Accounts Center. The export can include photos, comments, profile information, and other selected data for the Instagram profile you choose.

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

Meta's Accounts Center can manage more than one profile and product. Select the intended Instagram profile, then review the information scope and delivery settings before starting the export.

Current workflow

How to complete the current provider export

  1. 1

    In Instagram on the web, click More in the bottom left, then Settings.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  2. 2

    Open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  3. 3

    Click Create export, select the Instagram profile, then click Next.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  4. 4

    Select Export to device.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  5. 5

    Choose the information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  6. 6

    Click Start export.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  7. 7

    When Instagram notifies you, use Available downloads within 4 days to retrieve the protected export.

    Open the first-party step ↗

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Where do I request an Instagram data export?

On Instagram on the web, open More, Settings, Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, then Export your information.

Can I export only part of my Instagram information?

Yes. The current flow lets you choose the information, date range, format, notification email, and media quality.

How long is the Instagram download available?

The completed export remains under Available downloads for four days.
