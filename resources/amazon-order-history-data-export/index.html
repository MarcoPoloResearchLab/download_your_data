Current workflow

How to complete the current provider export

  1. 1

    Sign in to your Amazon account in a web browser and open Your Account from the navigation menu.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  2. 2

    Under Privacy & Security, select Request Your Information (or access Privacy Central directly).

    Open the first-party step ↗
  3. 3

    Select the data categories to export, including Your Orders for CSV purchase reports, Kindle content, and Prime Video watch history.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  4. 4

    Click Submit Request at the bottom of the data category selection list.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  5. 5

    Open the confirmation email sent by Amazon to your registered email address and click the verification link.

    Open the first-party step ↗
  6. 6

    Wait for Amazon to generate your archive, then download your data ZIP containing your CSV order history files.

    Open the first-party step ↗

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

How long does Amazon take to generate my data report?

Simple order history reports generate within minutes, while full personal data requests may take up to several days.

Does the Amazon export include Prime Video history?

Yes. When selected during the request flow, Amazon includes Prime Video titles and viewing activity.

In what format does Amazon provide order history data?

Amazon provides order history reports as downloadable CSV files.
