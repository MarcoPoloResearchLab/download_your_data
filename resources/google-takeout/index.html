Google (All Account Data) resource

How to Export Account Data with Google Takeout

Google Takeout lets you export data from the Google products you use, including Gmail, Drive, Photos, Maps, and YouTube. You control the included products, delivery method, frequency, file type, and archive size.

Quick verdict

Best for
Creating one configurable export across multiple Google products
Required input
A signed-in Google account and enough secure storage for the archive
Product fit
Download Your Data provides the public export guide but no general Takeout importer

The decision that matters

Choose the right export before you start

A broad Takeout archive can be much larger and more sensitive than expected. Review the selected products, choose a private delivery method, and set a practical split size before creating the export.

Objections and limits

What this resource does not promise

  • Large exports can be split into multiple archives.
  • Creating a Takeout archive does not delete the original data.

Questions before you export

Frequently asked questions

Does Google Takeout export every Google product?

Products you use and that hold your data are selected automatically, but you can uncheck products or refine supported product options before continuing.

Why did Google split my Takeout export?

Google splits an export when it exceeds the maximum archive size you selected.

Does creating a Google Takeout export delete my data?

No. Creating the archive makes a copy and does not delete the original data.
